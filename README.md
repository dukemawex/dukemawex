### 🔬 What I work on

**AI safety & interpretability** — I want models we can actually inspect, not just score.
- Mechanistic interpretability with Stochastic Parameter Decomposition — mechanism clustering on real grokked transformers ([`spd-real-transformer`](https://github.com/dukemawex/spd-real-transformer), [`spd-mechanism-clustering`](https://github.com/dukemawex/spd-mechanism-clustering), [`spd-causal-clustering`](https://github.com/dukemawex/spd-causal-clustering)).
- Cross-architecture universality of deception features ([`deception-feature-universality`](https://github.com/dukemawex/deception-feature-universality)).
- Reward generalization early-warning ([`reward-generalization-earlywarning`](https://github.com/dukemawex/reward-generalization-earlywarning)).

**Agentic security** — the reasoning process itself is becoming an attack surface.
- [`reasoning-monitor`](https://github.com/dukemawex/reasoning-monitor) — detecting injected/absorbed premises in agentic reasoning chains, with activation probes.
- [`eval-oversight`](https://github.com/dukemawex/eval-oversight) — catching evaluation gaming with independent, held-out oversight.
- [`Teger-ai`](https://github.com/dukemawex/Teger-ai) — a browser & email plugin that highlights social-engineering cues, explains its reasoning, and builds an open dataset of phishing patterns. *Supported by the OpenAI Cybersecurity Grant.*

**Applied AI for Africa** — technology should reach the field, not just the paper.
- 🌱 [`Cropie`](https://github.com/dukemawex/Cropie) — crop-disease early warning · [`SoilIQ`](https://github.com/dukemawex/SoilIQ) — soil-health intelligence · [`FarmVoice-AI`](https://github.com/dukemawex/FarmVoice-AI) — agricultural extension agent.
- 💧 Water quality & availability monitoring ([`water-quality-sentinel`](https://github.com/dukemawex/water-quality-sentinel), [`Africa-water-watch`](https://github.com/dukemawex/Africa-water-watch)).
- 🗺️ Blockchain-secured land registry & boundary-dispute resolution ([`-LandChain-Africa`](https://github.com/dukemawex/-LandChain-Africa)).

**And I ship product** — full-stack apps, agent harnesses, and tooling around all of the above.

### 🧭 How I think
- Understand before you optimize. A score you can't explain isn't safety.
- Name the trade-off, then make the call.
- Build the thing, measure it honestly, and write down what still doesn't work.
